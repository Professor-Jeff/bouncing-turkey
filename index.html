The Bouncing Turkey 🦃

A turkey hides in one of the boxes. Each turn, open a box. If the turkey isn't there, it jumps 1 box left or right.

A jump has to land cleanly inside the row. If jumping one way would carry the turkey past an end, it jumps the other way instead. If neither way fits, it stays where it is.
